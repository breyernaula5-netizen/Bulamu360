Privacy Policy

How Bulamu360 collects, uses, stores, and protects customer information, including health-related nutrition information.

Last updated: June 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Bulamu360, we may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number.
  • Age, sex, height, weight, waist measurement, activity level, goals, and food preferences.
  • Health-related information such as reported conditions, symptoms, allergies, medication context, pregnancy/life-stage information, and lab values you choose to enter.
  • Payment reference information submitted for manual payment verification.
  • Follow-up information such as progress, symptoms, disliked meals, food barriers, and questions.

2. Why We Use This Information

We use your information to:

  • Prepare personalised nutrition guidance and meal-plan outputs.
  • Review payment/order requests and approve or reject access.
  • Send order confirmations, approval emails, reminders, and follow-up messages.
  • Improve meal practicality, recipe quality, and customer support.
  • Maintain safety checks for conditions that need caution or clinician review.

3. Health Information

Bulamu360 may process sensitive health-related information because nutrition guidance depends on medical context, symptoms, allergies, pregnancy status, and lab values where provided. You should only provide information you are comfortable sharing for nutrition support.

4. How We Protect Information

Bulamu360 uses backend controls to protect private information. Recipe databases, admin dashboards, secret keys, and order records are not intended for public access. Admin access requires login. API keys are stored as environment variables, not public website code.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customer health information. Information may be shared only when needed to operate Bulamu360, such as email delivery, database hosting, order management, or where required by law. If a serious health concern is identified, Bulamu360 may recommend that you contact a clinician or emergency service.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Order and follow-up records may be stored so Bulamu360 can provide support, resend approved plans, review outcomes, and improve service quality. Records should be retained only for as long as reasonably needed for service, support, business, legal, or safety purposes.

7. Your Choices

You may request help reviewing, correcting, or deleting your information where reasonably possible. Some records may need to be kept for order, payment, safety, or legal reasons.

8. Security Limits

No online system is perfectly secure. Bulamu360 uses reasonable safeguards, but users should avoid sending highly sensitive information over insecure channels and should never share admin passwords, approval codes, or private account credentials publicly.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or information requests, contact Bulamu360 through the official contact channel provided by Breyer Naula or the Bulamu360 customer support channel.
