FAQ

Common questions

Is the tracker really free?

Yes. The tracker is free and needs no account. Personal plans from Breyer are the paid service.

Where is my tracker data stored?

On your own device, in your browser. It is only shared with your dietitian if you connect with your approval code and press “Share now”. You can export or delete it at any time.

Does it include Ugandan foods?

Yes. Matooke, beans, posho, millet, sorghum, dodo, nakati, mukene, groundnuts and more are built in. You can also search verified USDA foods and packaged products, or create your own foods and recipes.

Is Bulamu360 a medical diagnosis service?

No. Bulamu360 provides nutrition guidance and tracking. It does not replace emergency care, diagnosis, medication, or your clinician's advice.

How do I receive my personal plan?

After your payment reference is reviewed and approved, you receive an approval code or a secure plan link.

Can Bulamu360 help with diabetes, pregnancy, kidney concerns, or gut issues?

It can provide cautious nutrition support, but specialist conditions may require labs, clinician input, and dietitian review before targets are intensified.

Whether you count calories, macros or micronutrients, count on Bulamu360.
