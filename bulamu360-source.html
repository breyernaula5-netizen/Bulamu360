About Your Dietician

Hello, I'm Breyer Naula, Your Registered Dietician.

I am a Certified Registered Dietician and Nutritionist (RDN) with extensive clinical experience. Through Bulamu360, I bring hospital-quality, evidence-based nutritional guidance directly to you, from wherever you are in Uganda.

I don't believe in copy-paste diets or extreme fads. Every plan I build is around your body, your BMI, your conditions, your lifestyle, your clinical safety needs, and the real foods from your local market.

Whether you're managing diabetes, battling hypertension, struggling with weight, or feeding your family well, Bulamu360 delivers clinical-grade guidance that actually fits your life.

Enter any results you have. Leave blank what you don't have. Breyer (RDN) will interpret each one and integrate findings into your plan.
🏅 Sports and Athletic Performance
Tell Breyer about your sport. This information is used to build your fuelling strategy, recovery nutrition, and timing recommendations around your training schedule.
🧠 Mental Health and Nutrition
What you eat significantly affects how you feel, think, and cope. Breyer (RDN) will incorporate foods that support brain chemistry, gut health, and stress management into your plan. All information is kept strictly confidential.
Nutrition for mental health works alongside (not instead of) professional mental health support. If you are in crisis, please reach out to a counsellor, trusted person, or your physician.
🧪 Food Micronutrient Checker
Type any food - local or international - to see its complete micronutrient breakdown. Data sourced from USDA FoodData Central and FAO/INFOODS. Works for virtually any food.
💫 Vitality and Hormonal Wellness
Hormonal balance, energy levels, and overall vitality are deeply connected to nutrition. This section helps Breyer (RDN) tailor your plan to support your hormonal health, energy, and physical wellbeing. Your responses are completely private and confidential.
All information is strictly confidential and used only to help Breyer personalise your nutritional plan. Hormonal and vitality issues are very commonly linked to nutritional deficiencies that are fully addressable through targeted dietary changes.
🔒 The Intimate Wellness section is only available to adults aged 18 and above. Please confirm your age in the Personal Details section above.
🌸 Intimate Wellness - For Adults 18 and Above
Everything you share here is completely private and confidential. This information helps Breyer (RDN) build a nutrition plan that directly supports your intimate health, physical energy, hormonal balance, and overall sexual wellbeing.
All responses are strictly confidential. Sexual and intimate health nutrition is a legitimate area of clinical dietetics with strong evidence supporting dietary interventions.
🤰 Pregnancy and Lactation Nutrition
Nutritional needs change significantly during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Breyer (RDN) will build a plan specifically calibrated for your stage, your baby's needs, and any special circumstances.
🌸 Women's Cycle-Based Nutrition
Your nutritional needs shift significantly across your menstrual cycle. Tell us where you are and Breyer will adjust your recommendations accordingly.
Your cycle phase significantly affects iron needs, magnesium requirements, carbohydrate tolerance, and mood. Breyer will tailor your plan to your current phase.
