FAQ

Common questions before starting.

Is Bulamu360 a medical diagnosis service?

No. Bulamu360 provides nutrition guidance and planning support. It does not replace emergency care, diagnosis, medication, or your clinician's advice.

How do customers receive their plan?

After payment reference review and approval, the customer receives an approval code or secure plan link.

Can Bulamu360 help with diabetes, pregnancy, kidney concerns, or gut issues?

It can provide cautious nutrition support, but specialist conditions may require labs, clinician input, and dietitian review before intensifying targets.

Can I use local foods?

Yes. Bulamu360 is designed to work with practical local foods as well as selected global recipes where they make nutritional and culinary sense.
