Questions

Before you begin.

Bulamu360 is designed to support nutrition decisions with clarity, caution, and dignity.

Is Bulamu360 a diagnosis service?

No. Bulamu360 provides nutrition guidance and planning support. It does not replace diagnosis, medication, emergency care, or clinician advice.

How do I receive my plan?

After your payment reference is reviewed and approved, you receive a secure link, approval code, or email copy.

Can it support specialist conditions?

Yes, with caution. Concerns such as diabetes, blood pressure, pregnancy, kidney-review needs, and gut symptoms may require labs, clinician input, and careful review.

Can I use local foods?

Yes. Bulamu360 is designed around local foods, market access, household routines, and practical recipes where they add value.
